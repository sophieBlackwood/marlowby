Our Story

Marlowby was born from a simple observation: across America, countless small towns and vibrant cities are home to incredible artisans, family-owned micro-smokehouses, craft bakeries, and independent creators. Yet, most consumers outside these regions never get the chance to discover them.

Founded with a passion for regional culture and local entrepreneurship, Marlowby set out to bridge the gap between everyday consumers and small-town makers. We partner directly with independent businesses in a different U.S. city each month—from the live music hubs of Austin, Texas to regional hidden gems. By curating monthly discovery boxes filled with specialty goods, regional flavors, and hand-crafted keepsakes, we share the unique identity and stories of America’s communities with subscribers nationwide.
